Heirloom, a flower breeding garden

Nine plots and a seed tray. Tab moves between them. Enter picks up a plant or a seed and Enter again drops it: onto another plant to cross them, onto the same plant to self it, onto a plot to sow a seed, onto another seed to splice them. C clones the plant you are on. R reads its field notebook card. Escape cancels, or closes the card.

A plant's traits are not named until it has finished growing, and seeds are never named. Growing the plant is how you find out what it is.
